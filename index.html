CS 203: Software tools and techniques for AI

January 2026 · IIT Gandhinagar

The teaching material for CS 203: how to collect, validate, label and augment data; how to call LLMs and evaluate and tune models; and the engineering that keeps them reproducible, monitored, fast and useful. Every lecture's slides, labs, notebooks, cheat sheet and short videos are listed below. The schedule, deadlines and recordings are on the course website, and the source for this page is in the GitHub repo.

Each row is one week's lecture, grouped by part of the course. The Shorts are also collected, in course order, on the Software tools for AI in 1 minute page and in a YouTube playlist.

Slides: the lecture slides as a web page (HTML) or PDF · Notebook: the worked lecture demo · Lab: the hands-on exercise, some of which open in Colab · Cheat sheet: a two-page A4 summary (PDF) · Shorts: one-minute videos, each answering one question; point at a number to see the question.
